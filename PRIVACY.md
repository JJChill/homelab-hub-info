# Privacy policy: Homelab hub

Homelab hub is a personal app used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

- **What it accesses:** the owner's Google Calendar, read-only (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly`). It cannot create, change or delete events.
- **What it does with it:** event details are fetched and shown to the owner on their own device. They are kept in memory only and are not written to logs.
- **Sharing:** no calendar data is sent to, sold to, or shared with any third party. There are no other users.
- **Revoking access:** the owner can remove access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
